Stats Guide & What They Do

        
      

      

        
Player Attributes (0–100)

        
INI — Initiative: how fast a fighter acts and reacts at the start of each exchange; boosts early offense.

        
STR — Strike Potency: raw impact; increases push and zone gain when hits land.

        
PRC — Precision: accuracy and targeting consistency; pairs with composure for clean hits.

        
GST — Guard Stability: structure of blocks/parries; reduces enemy push.

        
AWR — Awareness: reads lanes and timing; aids counters and reduces damage from glancing hits.

        
RHY — Rhythm: keeps internal tempo steady; smooths spikes and helps sustain pressure.

        
CMP — Composure: mental steadiness; improves defense and accuracy under pressure.

        
POS — Position Sense: spacing, lane discipline, micro-rotations; stabilizes formation.

        
STM — Stamina: short-term energy pool; drains with exertion.

        
END — Endurance: how well performance holds as stamina dips.


        
Round Controls

        
Ticks: how many simulation steps the round runs. Δt: seconds per step. Ticks × Δt = round length.

        
Scale: dampens how fast the zone meter moves. Bigger = calmer movement.

        
Variance: ±randomness % injected into offense/defense each tick for unpredictability.

        
Seed: same seed = same random sequence; change it for a different “roll.”


        
Under the Hood (simplified)

        
Each tick, both sides compute Offense (avg of STR/PRC/INI/RHY × stamina × composure) and Defense (avg of GST/AWR/CMP/POS × stamina × composure). The difference becomes momentum, which nudges the zone meter toward blue or red.

        
Stamina and composure shift dynamically: pushing drains more stamina; losing ground chips composure; steady play can recover both a little.
