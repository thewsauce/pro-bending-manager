Round Controls

        
Δt sets tick spacing; Ticks auto = 180 / Δt (3:00 cap). Scale calms the meter (bigger = slower zone movement). Variance = ±randomness. Seed = deterministic randomness.

        
Under the Hood (simplified)

        
Each tick: Offense = avg(STR, PRC, INI, RHY) × Stamina × Composure; Defense = avg(GST, AWR, CMP, POS) × Stamina × Composure. Off−Def → momentum → zone drift. Pushing drains STM; swings nudge CMP. Between rounds: small recovery.
